# DECISION FRAMEWORKS & HEURISTICS
*Archon Strategic Architecture -- Seneca Advisor*

---

## 1. REVERSIBILITY CLASSIFICATION (TYPE 1 VS TYPE 2)
*Heuristic derived from Jeff Bezos / Amazon Leadership Principles.*

### Type 1: Irreversible Decisions ("One-Way Doors")
- **Characteristics**: Extremely difficult or prohibitively expensive to unwind once executed (e.g., selling equity, signing long-term commercial leases, publishing proprietary IP, burning relationships).
- **Protocol**: Move deliberately. Seek diverse perspectives. Enforce cooling-off periods (minimum 48-72 hours). Demand rigorous pre-mortems and negative consequence analysis.

### Type 2: Reversible Decisions ("Two-Way Doors")
- **Characteristics**: Can be reversed, undone, or pivoted with low operational cost and minimal reputational fallout (e.g., pricing experiment, copy variations, zero-dependency helper scripts, draft PRs).
- **Protocol**: Optimize for velocity. Move fast with 70% information. Never let refinement substitute for execution on Type 2 choices.

---

## 2. REGRET MINIMIZATION FRAMEWORK
- Project forward to age 80.
- Ask: *"Looking back on this choice from decades ahead, which option minimizes cumulative regret?"*
- Distinguishes between transient social friction/fear of failure (which fades quickly) and the lingering ache of unattempted potential.

---

## 3. SECOND-ORDER THINKING & CHESTERTON'S FENCE
- **First-Order**: "What is the immediate, direct consequence of this action?"
- **Second-Order**: "And then what? What are the behavioral adaptations, systemic reactions, and countermoves by competitors or market participants over 6-24 months?"
- **Chesterton's Fence**: Never demolish a process, constraint, or boundary until you thoroughly understand why it was erected in the first place.

---

## 4. INVERSION & STOIC PRE-MORTEM (MORTALITER)
- Instead of asking *"How do we guarantee success?"*, invert the problem: *"How could this initiative fail catastrophically?"*
- Brainstorm all probable paths to collapse (burnout, distraction, over-engineering, regulatory block, cash depletion).
- Systematically erect guardrails and invariant constraints to eliminate each fatal mode before investing capital or time.

---

## 5. STRATEGIC ABANDONMENT & SUNK COST RUBRIC
- Sunk costs are historical data points; they have zero bearing on prospective return on investment.
- Ask: *"Knowing what I know today, if I were not already invested in this project, would I invest my next 100 hours into starting it?"*
- If the honest answer is No, define a structured wind-down or deliberate pivot.
