# 2026 PLATFORM ALGORITHMS & ORGANIC DISTRIBUTION
*Archon Growth & Content Architecture -- Orwell Advisor*

---

## 1. X / TWITTER (2026 HEURISTICS)
- **Primary Signals**:
  - Dwell Time (>12 seconds on text/media) is the highest weighted retention indicator.
  - Bookmark-to-Impression ratio out-indexes retweets and simple likes by 4.2x.
  - Replies from verified/high-reputation accounts within first 20 minutes provide 8x velocity multiplier.
- **Algorithm Penalties**:
  - External links in the primary post body trigger a 40-60% reach suppression penalty.
  - Self-contained technical breakdowns with diagrams or code snippets achieve maximal organic reach.
  - Engagement pods or repetitive copy triggers instant shadow-filtering.

---

## 2. LINKEDIN
- **Primary Signals**:
  - First-hour comment velocity from relevant industry practitioners.
  - Meaningful dialogue: Comments longer than 15 words receive 3x algorithm boost.
  - Document / PDF carousels and substantive engineering essays retain high dwell time.
- **Key Directive**: Avoid superficial corporate praise ("thrilled to announce"). Open with vulnerability, technical friction, or counter-intuitive system design lessons.

---

## 3. REDDIT & HACKER NEWS
- **Reddit Invariants**:
  - Subreddit culture first: Never post marketing copy. Post raw code, post-mortems, or architecture blueprints.
  - Strict 9:1 rule: 9 value-add community comments for every 1 self-referential post.
- **Hacker News Invariants**:
  - Title simplicity: Strict factual title (e.g. "Show HN: Zero-dependency local memory engine in Python").
  - Instant death: Any marketing superlatives ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless") trigger immediate flagging and penalty filtering.
