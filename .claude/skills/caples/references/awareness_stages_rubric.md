# AWARENESS STAGES RUBRIC
*Eugene Schwartz Framework Applied to Modern Technical Copywriting -- Caples Advisor*

---

## 1. THE 5 STAGES OF CUSTOMER AWARENESS

### Stage 1: Most Aware
- **Customer Mindset**: Knows your product, knows what it does, trusts your brand, only needs the offer details.
- **Copy Angle**: Direct, crisp announcement. Price, terms, deadline, or immediate CTA.
- **Headline Formula**: "[Product] is now live. Here is how to get started."

### Stage 2: Product-Aware
- **Customer Mindset**: Knows your product exists and knows what alternatives exist, but isn't convinced your specific implementation is the best choice.
- **Copy Angle**: Superiority, differentiation, benchmark proofs, concrete performance metrics.
- **Headline Formula**: "Why [Competitor/Alternative] users are switching to [Product]."

### Stage 3: Solution-Aware
- **Customer Mindset**: Knows the category of solution they need (e.g. "I need an observability platform" or "I need an MCP auditing tool"), but doesn't know your specific product.
- **Copy Angle**: Categorical leadership, unique mechanism, workflow improvement.
- **Headline Formula**: "The modern way to [achieve outcome] without [painful friction]."

### Stage 4: Problem-Aware
- **Customer Mindset**: Feels the visceral pain of a problem, but doesn't know solutions exist.
- **Copy Angle**: Agitate the frustration, validate their experience, then introduce the category.
- **Headline Formula**: "Tired of [specific symptom]? Here is what is actually causing it."

### Stage 5: Completely Unaware
- **Customer Mindset**: Has no knowledge of the problem or category; just living their life or doing their work.
- **Copy Angle**: Counter-intuitive story, startling industry data, provocative human observation.
- **Headline Formula**: "The invisible cost of [common industry default]."
