# POSITIONING FRAMEWORK
*April Dunford Methodology for Developer & SaaS Products -- Caples Advisor*

---

## 1. THE 5 ESSENTIAL POSITIONING COMPONENTS

1. **Competitive Alternatives**:
   - If your product disappeared tomorrow, what would users genuinely do? (Spreadsheets, manual scripting, hiring a freelancer, or ignoring the issue).
2. **Unique Attributes**:
   - What capabilities do you have that alternatives objectively lack? (e.g., zero pip dependencies, runs 100% offline, local JSONL storage).
3. **Value & Proof**:
   - So what? What tangible business or developer benefit does that unique attribute unlock? (Instant execution, zero security supply-chain risk).
4. **Target Customer Segment**:
   - Who cares intensely about that value right now? (Technical founders and engineering leads who have been burned by dependency rot).
5. **Market Category**:
   - What context makes your value obvious? (e.g., "A zero-dependency developer toolkit" vs "An enterprise AI platform").

---

## 2. THE CORE POSITIONING STATEMENT
```text
For [target customer]
who [statement of need / critical frustration],
[Product Name] is a [market category]
that [primary benefit / compelling value proposition].
Unlike [primary competitive alternative],
[Product Name] delivers [unique differentiator with verifiable proof].
```
